One mark per kind of work

Each mark says what is running and that it is running. The main conversation, subagents, forks, shells and monitors each get a shape of their own that moves while the work goes on and stands still when it stops. Every motion is steady: nothing waits, fades or blinks.

The recommended set

Running on the left, stopped on the right, then at the sizes you will see in the app: 11px on cards and status bars, 9px on sidebar sub-lines and past rows.

Options for each kind

The main conversation and subagents are designed in pairs, because a subagent is a smaller copy of the agent: in each pair the subagent carries the agent’s moving mark as its head. Forks, shells and monitors stay distinct from that family. Each option shows the running mark at 64px, then at 11px and 9px, then the still mark.

Kind and status together

When work stops or needs you, the kind mark stands still and a status badge takes its bottom right corner. The badge uses the status shapes you already know.

What to check

  • Every moving mark is running, and its shape says what kind. Nothing else on the page moves, apart from the red needs-you stripes.
  • The agent spark and the subagent’s spark-head turn the same way: one family, told apart by the shoulders.
  • Past rows show the still kind mark with a status badge, so a failed shell and a failed subagent differ at a glance.
  • Session marks in the sidebar use the agent mark: the spark while it works, the still spark with a badge when it waits for you.
  • Kind colour sits on the card edge and in the word, as in the app today.

Squint test

The same panel and sidebar in grey and out of focus, roughly what you see at the edge of your vision or without colour. Running items should still differ by shape and motion: a turning spark, a turning head on shoulders, beads running up a branch, a cursor running along a prompt, a hand sweeping a ring.
