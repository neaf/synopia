What to check

  • Red rows first: the stripes are the only marks that move up and down.
  • Turning marks are busy; still marks have stopped or are waiting for you at leisure.
  • The blue badges stay on To review until you mark them reviewed, so they never scroll into Past.
  • Kind tags sit after the status mark, pale and still. Read them second.
  • One banner explains the AWS locks, instead of opening each session.

Squint test

The same sidebars and panel in grey and out of focus, roughly what you see at the edge of your vision or without red and green. Every row should still be told apart by shape and motion: turning ring, seesawing stripes, still dot with a moving satellite, backwards dashes, solid square, half ring that does not move.

Rules

What to keep in mind when adding a new state.

Each shape means one thing

  • Circle: Claude is doing something. It turns while it works; the variants (dashes, inner dot, rings, hand) say how.
  • Two stripes: paused because it needs you. The only mark that moves up and down.
  • Solid dot: Claude finished and nothing blocks you. Amber, and still.
  • Square: it stopped before it was done. Grey when you stopped it, red when it failed, an outline when time ran out.
  • Check: done, nothing for you to look at.
  • Blue means yours: your queued message, a deliverable you asked for.
  • Tinted tag: what kind of background work it is. A separate, quieter layer: pale tint, no motion, always after the status mark.
  • Lock: the only mark that names its cause, because one sign-in fixes every session at once.

Motions stay apart

No two moving marks share both shape and motion. Speed alone is never the only difference.

MotionSpeed

Everything else is still. Turning means Claude is busy; up and down means it is waiting for you.

Shared on purpose

  • Question, permission and plan approval share the stripes. The status text says which; at 12px three variants would not read apart, and all three ask the same of you: go and answer.
  • Thinking and running a tool share the arc. They swap every few seconds, so a different mark would flicker. Starting up uses it too.
  • Subagent, fork and shell share the running arc. The kind tag next to it tells them apart, so the status mark never has to.
  • Done in the background and Looks finished share the check: both mean nothing is left to do.
  • Failed is the same red square for sessions and background work.
