Why it changed

The old card had no grid, said everything twice and gave every line the same weight, so the decision was hard to find.

  1. The question was shown twice.The transcript repeated the question and its options, then the card showed them again with other numbers.
  2. The turn looked finished.“Files changed” and “Worked for 1h 18m, done” came after the open question.
  3. The status said nothing specific.“Claude is asking you something” hid that there were two questions and how many were answered.
  4. Boxes inside a panel.Six bordered boxes of equal weight made the card about 560 pixels tall, with every question open at once.
  5. Numbers you couldn't type.“2.1” suggested a chord, but number keys only reached the first question.

The design: one question open at a time

Each question is one line: a status mark, its header, and the answer or “Not answered”. Only the current question is open, with Claude's text and the option rows. A red header replaces the status line and counts the answers. The closed lines are the review, so there is no separate review step.

Nothing answered yet. The cursor is on the first option of Mark ready.

How it works

  • Marks: ✓ answered, ● current, ○ not answered.
  • j k move the cursor › through the open question's options, Space picks. A single-choice pick opens the next unanswered question.
  • h l open the previous or next question; clicking a line does the same.
  • “Something else” is the last row. i types it in the message box and Enter saves it.
  • The message box only shows the mode by its border: grey in normal mode, a blue ring in insert mode. Send lives in the card's footer.

Decided against

  • All questions open at once as one stacked list.
  • Horizontal tabs for the questions, and questions side by side.
  • Number keys to pick (“1 2 3 4 pick”). Numbers do nothing in the card.
  • A separate review step before sending.
  • Mode labels in the message box and a Send button in it.
  • Repeating the question in the transcript while it waits.

Permission prompt

The same card with one open item: the command and Claude's reason, then option rows. The last row is your own words, “Deny” with a reason typed in the message box. With one item there is no header line, no progress and no h l.

Permission to run a command.

Plan approval

The plan itself is the last message in the transcript. The card holds only the decision, with “Ask for changes” as the type-your-own row.

The end of the transcript while Claude waits

Files changed comes first, then one waiting line points at the message panel. The question is not repeated, and no “Worked for” line follows until the turn really ends.

Rules

  • One question open at a time.Every other question is one line: mark, header, answer or “Not answered”. The lines are the review.
  • The header replaces the status line.“Needs you, Claude asks N questions, for X min” and “k of N answered”. It is the only red block on screen.
  • One place for the decision.The transcript gets one waiting line, never a copy of the question or its options.
  • The waiting line is last.Files changed goes before it; “Worked for” waits for the end of the turn.
  • One structure for every kind.Questions, permissions and plans all use one open item with option rows. Reading material, like a plan, goes in the transcript.
  • Your own words are the last row.“Something else”, “Deny” or “Ask for changes”, always typed in the message box.
  • Rows, not boxes.Cursor, mark, label, description on one grid. Only the picked row gets a background; the cursor row gets a thin blue outline.
  • The border is the mode.Grey message box in normal mode, blue ring in insert mode. No mode label, no Send button in the box.
  • Red means needs you, blue means picked.Recommended is a green word under the label. The current question's header is blue.
  • Claude's words in mono, Synopia's in sans.Questions, options and commands are mono like the transcript. The header, hints and buttons are sans.

Keys in normal mode while Claude waits

j kMove the cursor through the open question's options, including the type-your-own row.
SpacePick. Single choice: picks and opens the next unanswered question. Multiple choice: toggles and stays.
h lOpen the previous or next question.
iType your own answer in the message box. Enter saves it and opens the next unanswered question; Esc goes back.
EnterSend, once every question has an answer. Before that it opens the next unanswered question.
J KSwitch sessions, as always.
1 to 9Not used for picking.
